## Breakers of E 2.1.1

Two-sided tokens. Many tokens have another token printed on the back (Commander decks since 2014, boosters since Modern Horizons, and every Reality Fracture token), but Scryfall lists each side as its own token. Now BoE knows they're one card.

### What's new
- **Adding a token asks what's on its other side.** Edit → Pool → Collection → Tokens: add a token and the **Other Side** window lists the card pool's tokens of that release, all its token sets. Pick the one on the back and **Add to Collection**: both go in, paired, in the same finish, language and condition. No other side: **Single-Sided** (Enter). Tokens from before two-sided ones existed (2012) and Scryfall's own double-faced tokens don't ask.
- **One card, two rows.** Each side keeps its own row and shows the other: **Treasure // Angel** and **Angel // Treasure**. The totals count each card once ("19 rows = 10 cards").
- **Decks.** A deck that takes one side takes the card: the other side shows as Used, and its "Used in" list names the deck. The same deck can hold both sides of one card.
- **Show Other Side** in the card panel and the card window shows the token on the back.
- **Removing one side removes the other**; changing finish, language or condition moves both. Undo puts both back, paired.
- **Fix a pair** with the **Two-Sided…** button or right-click → **Other Side…**, and take one apart with right-click → **Unpair**.
- **Share pairs** with your group: right-click → **Export / Import Two-Sided Pairs…** (a CSV file of names, sets and collector numbers).
- **Suggested Tokens** finds a token on either side of your two-sided cards, and on either face of a double-faced token. **Prefer** picks single-sided or two-sided first (remembered). One card of yours that's both tokens a deck makes is used for both, one side at a time.
- **Print slips** of a two-sided token print both sides (Side 1 of 2, Side 2 of 2), or tick **Print one side only**.
- **Guided Build**: a pick past the guide's target says "over the guide" instead of "(9 of 8)".
- A new Help topic, **Two-Sided Tokens**.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.1.1.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection and decks stay as they are. Nothing is paired until you pair it: BoE never guesses which tokens share a card, since pairings differ between boosters and decks.
