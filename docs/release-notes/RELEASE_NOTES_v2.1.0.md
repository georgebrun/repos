## Breakers of E 2.1.0

The deck builder: Commander and 60-card decks drafted from the cards you own, with a reason for every card. Plus everything it needed: card tags, combos, EDHREC, salt scores, a Want List per deck, print slips and deck versions.

### What's new
- **Build a deck.** Edit → Decks → **Build** drafts a deck from your collection, and every card says why it's in.
  - **Commander** (partners and Backgrounds included), or **60-card** for the format you pick: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and more.
  - Use **only your free copies**, or your collection plus **cards to get** within a spend limit. An optional cap keeps the whole deck under a value.
  - It aims for the **Commander Bracket** you pick: Game Changers, two-card combos, land denial and extra turns.
  - A **guide** (lands, ramp, card draw, removal, board wipes …) you can change or switch off. 60-card decks also get a **mana curve**, **key cards** to build around and **plan words**.
  - **Lock** or **Exclude** cards and build again; **Cancel** stops a build at any time.
  - **Guided Build** lets you pick the cards for each job yourself from the best options.
  - **What can I build?** ranks your commanders, partner pairs or color combinations by how much of a deck you already own.
  - **Accept** makes the deck: your free copies are claimed, the rest go on the Want List for it, the runner-ups go in its sideboard as **swap options**, and its tokens are offered (print them or add them).
  - A **Rule 0 sheet** to share with your playgroup, from the draft or from Deck Statistics.
- **Deck versions.** Edit → Decks → **Versions…**: save a version with a note, compare any two (or one with the deck now), restore one. A version is saved when the builder makes a deck and before every restore.
- **Swap options in the sideboard.** Cards there aren't claimed. **Move to Main Deck** claims your free copy (or puts it on the Want List); **Move to Sideboard** frees it.
- **Deck Statistics** gets a **Combos** tab (in the deck and one card away), an **EDHREC** tab for the commander, the **saltiest cards** on Overview and a real two-card combo check on **Bracket**.
- **Card tags, links and salt.** The card panel and card window show Scryfall's card tags, the card's EDHREC salt score, and links to EDHREC, Scryfall and Commander Spellbook. On the Edit pages, right-click → **Open on ▸**.
- **Update Database** also downloads Scryfall's card tags, Commander Spellbook's combos and EDHREC's salt scores.
- **A Want List per deck.** Every want is **for** a deck or for your **Collection** (the new **For** column). Add to it from the card pool, from Edit → Collection, or from a deck's missing cards. **Got It** puts the copies in your collection and claims them for the decks they were for, with a tick for each want.
- **Tear Down asks about the Want List.** A deck's wants: tick the ones to keep for the Collection; the rest are removed.
- **Print slips.** Print token slips from a deck's Suggested Tokens, and card slips (marked PROXY) from the Want List and a deck's table.
- **View → Decks** counts the main deck only; a sideboard shows on the tile's second line.
- **What's New** in Help lists all of this.

### Fixed
- Exports leave Oversized cards out unless you tick them.
- A second Update Database right after the first no longer fails while the keyword list is still being rebuilt; it waits for it.
- The update summary keeps the card data results when the rulings finish.
- The card panel's "Available as" showed Unknown for Want List and Trade Binder rows.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.1.0.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection and decks stay as they are. Then run **Update Database** once, so the card tags, combos and salt scores download.
