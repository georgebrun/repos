## Breakers of E 2.1.3

Your phone lists, hands-free: connect Dropbox once, and your phone always has your newest Trade Binder and Want List.

### What's new
- **Connect Dropbox…** in **Settings → Phone lists** (or **📱 Phone…** on the Trade Binder and Want List). Sign in on Dropbox's page and click Allow. From then on BoE sends your lists to your Dropbox whenever your collection changes. No Dropbox app is needed on the PC.
- **The BoE Lists page** ([georgebrun.github.io/repos/lists](https://georgebrun.github.io/repos/lists/)): tap **Sign in with Dropbox** once, with the same Dropbox. After that it gets your newest lists by itself every time you open it, with no file picking and no Update. **Refresh** gets them right now; with no signal it shows the lists kept on the phone.
- **Private.** BoE only gets its own folder in your Dropbox (Apps › Breakers of E Lists) and never sees your other files. **Disconnect** in Settings or **Sign out of Dropbox** on the phone stops it.
- **No Dropbox yet?** A **Free Dropbox account** link with the steps, in BoE and on the phone page. Dropbox Basic is free.
- The cloud drive folder way still works, for anyone who'd rather not use Dropbox.
- A new How-To, **Scan Cards with Your Phone**: scan with the free ManaBox app and import its file. Every card comes in as the exact printing and finish you scanned.

### Fixed
- The Other Side window no longer shows a sideways scroll bar it doesn't need.
- Suggested Tokens now claims a foil copy you already own, instead of adding a non-foil line.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.1.3.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection, decks and phone lists stay as they are.
