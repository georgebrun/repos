## Breakers of E 2.1.2

Your Trade Binder and Want List on your phone, iPhone or Android, for the store or a trade.

### What's new
- **📱 Phone…** on the Trade Binder and Want List (it also shows by itself the first time, until you tick "Don't show this again"), or **Settings → Phone lists**. Tick **Keep my Trade Binder and Want List on my phone**: BoE writes **BoE Lists.json** to a folder in your cloud drive (OneDrive\Breakers of E to start; **Change Folder…** for Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox) and writes it again by itself whenever your collection changes. **Update Now** writes it straight away.
- **The BoE Lists page** ([georgebrun.github.io/repos/lists](https://georgebrun.github.io/repos/lists/)): scan the QR code in Settings, add the page to your Home Screen, open your lists file once. Trade Binder and Want List tabs, card pictures (from Scryfall), the full set name, search, filters by color or deck, sorting, prices and your asking prices, and a tap for the card's details. It keeps the lists on the phone, so it works without signal; tap **Update** and pick the file again for newer lists.
- **Private and free.** The page is the same for everyone and holds nobody's cards; yours stay in your own cloud drive. Only the Trade Binder and Want List go in the file, never the rest of your collection or your decks.
- A new Help topic, **Phone Lists**.

### Fixed
- Open Folder in Settings → Phone lists opens File Explorer at the file, OneDrive folders included.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.1.2.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection and decks stay as they are. Phone lists are off until you turn them on in Settings.

Your cloud drive's desktop app (OneDrive, Google Drive for desktop, iCloud for Windows or Dropbox) must be installed and signed in on the PC, so the file reaches your phone.
