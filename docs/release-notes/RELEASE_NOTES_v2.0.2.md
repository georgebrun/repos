## Breakers of E 2.0.2

Sets grouped by release, a cleaner gallery, a faster way to add the packs you open, and MTGO and Arena import and export.

### What's new
- **Sets by release.** View → Sets shows each release as one tile: the set with its Commander decks, promos, tokens, art series, oversized and front cards. Click it for the release's page, with every part and how much of it you own.
- **Edit → Sets.** Opened a pack, a box or a precon? Open the set, go down its pictures and click **+** on what you got. Each part goes to its own collection by itself: cards to Cards, tokens to Tokens, oversized cards to Oversized, and so on. **Missing Only** and dimmed pictures show what you still need.
- **Cleaner gallery.** Nothing covers the card picture any more. Underneath, each finish the card comes in has its own column: the price of one copy (hover it for the total) and how many you have. Gold is foil and etched. On the Edit pages each column has its own **− / +**, so no more Shift or Ctrl clicks, and with both tables in Gallery the big Add / Remove buttons step aside.
- **A finish that doesn't exist is flagged.** If you have copies in a finish Scryfall doesn't list for that printing, its column turns amber: usually the wrong set or number was picked.
- **MTGO and Arena import and export.**
  - Import Magic Online .dek and CSV files (matched exactly by MTGO id, foils included) and Arena or MTGO text lists into your online collections.
  - Export the MTGO collection as a .dek or MTGO CSV, and the Arena collection as Arena text.
  - Send a deck to Arena (paste into its Import) or MTGO (.dek). Cards are swapped for a printing that game has, and anything it doesn't have is listed.
- **Replace imports back up first.** Every Import → Replace saves a fresh copy of your collection in the Backups folder.

### Fixed
- In exports, a deck's commander now goes under **Commander**, not with the rest of the deck.
- The set checklist counted cards you own only as etched as missing.
- In a set's gallery, a card you just added stayed dimmed until you left the set.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.0.2.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection and decks stay as they are. Then run **Update Database** once, so the Sets page can group sets by release.
