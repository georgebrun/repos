Breakers of E on your PC keeps your lists up to date in your Dropbox or in a file, BoE Lists.json, in your cloud drive. They're kept on this phone, so they work without signal too.

Hands-free: the same Dropbox as Breakers of E, once. After that this page gets your newest lists by itself.

No Dropbox yet? Free Dropbox account
Dropbox Basic is free. Sign up with your name, email and a password, and confirm the email Dropbox sends. Skip any offer of a paid trial or the Dropbox app; you don't need either. Then connect it in Breakers of E on your PC (Settings → Phone Lists → Connect Dropbox…) and come back here to sign in.
