# Build a Deck
> Edit → Decks → Build: a Commander or 60-card deck drafted from your collection, every card with its reason.

**Deck type** at the top: **Commander (100 cards)** or **60-card (Constructed)**. Each keeps its own guide numbers. Switching clears locks and excludes (they were for the other kind of deck).

## What you tell it
- **What can I build?…**: your commanders and the partner pairs you own (Partner, Friends forever, a Background …), best first, by how much of a deck your free cards already make for them: free spells in their colors, how far those go toward the guide, and (when EDHREC's page for the commander is saved) how many of its 100 most-played cards you have free. **Build This One** picks it (both commanders for a pair). Tick **Include commanders whose copies are in a deck** to see those too.
- **Commander**: type to search. The list is A→Z and says which are free, yours but in use, or not owned.
- **Second commander** (shown when the commander can have one): Partner, Partner with, Friends forever, Choose a Background, or Doctor's companion. Only cards that can pair with the first are listed. **✕** for none. Both commanders' colors count.
- **Bracket**: 1 Exhibition to 5 cEDH (default 3). It keeps to the bracket: Game Changers (none in 1–2, up to 3 in 3), no mass land denial or chained extra turns below 4, and no two-card infinite combos below 4.
- **Cards to use**:
  - **Only my collection's free copies** (default): nothing to buy. Copies a deck or the Trade Binder already uses aren't touched.
  - **My collection + cards to get**: also cards you don't own, up to **Spend up to $** in all. They go on the Want List when you accept.
- **Deck worth at most $** (optional): the whole deck's value, your cards included.
- **Use the guide**: about how many of each kind of card: lands, ramp, card draw, removal, board wipes, protection, counterspells, tutors, recursion and finishers. Only a guide: change any number, untick any line, or untick **Use the guide** for just the best cards.

## 60-card decks
- **Format**: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and the rest. Only cards legal in it are used; Vintage's restricted cards get 1 copy.
- **Colors**: tick the deck's colors. **M** (multicolor) and **N** (non-color: colorless artifacts and the like) are on to start; untick them to leave those spells out. With no colors ticked and N on, it builds a colorless deck. Spells are matched by their mana cost (a hybrid symbol needs one of its colors; a Phyrexian one can be paid with life), lands by their colors. Cards that only work with a commander (Command Tower, Arcane Signet …) are left out.
- **Key cards**: type a card's name and **Add** (or Enter; a name left in the box counts when you build). It gets as many copies as the format allows (4), its colors are ticked, and its text sets the plan (a creature type it cares about — "Clerics you control", "the number of Elves" — counts as a theme). ✕ takes it off.
- **Plan words**: words the cards for your plan have, separated by commas: token, +1/+1 counter, Elf, artifact … Cards with them fit the deck.
- **Power**: **Casual**, or **Competitive** (combos, efficient cards, one more copy of each).
- **What can I build?…** (60-card): the color combinations — each color, every pair and three-color set, and colorless — ranked by your free cards legal in the format: spell copies (up to 4 each), playsets, the guide's roles, and the creature type most of them share (a tribe to build around). **Build This One** ticks those colors.
- **Mana curve**: **Low** (fast, cheap spells), **Medium**, **High** (slower, bigger spells), or no target. The notes under the draft show the curve it got against the target.
- **The guide** counts cards, copies included: 24 lands, 8 removal, 6 card draw, 4 finishers, 4 ramp, 2 board wipes. Counterspells, protection, tutors and recursion start off; tick them for decks that want them.
- **Copies**: cheap cards get 4, mid-cost 3, expensive ones 2 (board wipes 2). Only your free copies count with **Only my collection's free copies**; with **cards to get**, it buys the rest of a playset within **Spend up to $**. **Source** says "2 yours + 2 to get" when it's both.
- EDHREC is for commander decks, so it isn't used here; Commander Spellbook's combos are.
- **Accept…** makes a Constructed deck in that format. Locking a card keeps its copies.

## The draft
Double-click a card in the draft (or the card panel's picture) for the larger card window; **Prev** / **Next** go through the draft. Guided Build's options open it the same way.

Click **Build Draft**. While it works, a bar shows what it's doing; **Cancel** (or Esc) stops it and keeps the last draft. The commander is first (both, with partners), then the rest. Each card shows:
- **Source**: **Free (yours)**, **To get $x**, or **Basic**.
- **Why**: what it does in the deck (the guide line it fills), where it comes from, how many of this commander's decks on EDHREC play it, and how it fits the commander.

Above the draft:
- The guide's lines, green when met, amber when short. "+9 more cards do it too" counts cards picked for other reasons that do that job as well.
- Notes: a role it couldn't fill, combos in the draft, and what it had to go on (your collection, card tags, EDHREC, combos).

It picks from what EDHREC says this commander's decks play, the commander's own themes, combos with cards already in, cards that do more than one job, mana cost, and your free copies first. Without card tags (Update Database), it reads what cards do from their rules text, which is rougher.

## Guided Build
**Guided Build…** builds it step by step: one step per guide line (Ramp, Card draw …), then **The deck's plan**. Each step lists the best options for that job with the reason for each, with what you've chosen so far already counted. The top ones are ticked to start; tick and untick what you like, then **Next** (**Back** to change an earlier step).
- Cards chosen in another step that do this job count toward it too.
- It won't let you go over the bracket's Game Changers or the deck's spell slots.
- **Finish — fill the rest** (any step): your choices are locked and the builder fills the rest, lands included.

## Change it
- Right-click cards → **Lock (always keep it)**: they stay in every rebuild.
- Right-click cards → **Exclude and rebuild**: they're left out, and the draft is built again.
- Change anything on the left and click **Build Draft** again. **Clear locks and excludes** starts over.

## Swap options
**Swap options** (default 12, up to 30; 0 for none): the best cards that just missed the deck, taken in turns from each guide line and the plan, so every job has a spare or two. They're listed after the deck in the draft, in the accent color and italics, and marked **Swap** in Source, with their reasons. Lock one to put it in the deck.

**Accept** puts them in the new deck's **sideboard**, unclaimed: your free copies stay free for other decks. After testing, right-click one on the deck page → **Move to Main Deck** to swap it in (your free copy is claimed, or it goes on the Want List), and move the card it replaces to the sideboard (its copies are freed). In a Commander deck the sideboard isn't part of the deck, so it doesn't count anywhere.

## Try it, then keep it
- **Suggested Tokens…**: the tokens the draft's cards make, in the same window as Edit → Decks. Look and **Print Ticked…** now; adding waits for Accept.
- **Statistics & Test Hands…**: Deck Statistics for the draft: mana, combos, bracket, EDHREC, and **Sample Hand** to draw test hands.
- **Rule 0 Sheet…**: what to tell the table before a game: commander, the bracket you aimed for and the bracket the cards show, Game Changers, two-card combos, extra turns, land denial, tutors, interaction, how it wins and the saltiest cards. **Copy** or **Save…** it as a text file.
- **Accept…**: name it, and BoE saves it as a new deck: your free copies are claimed for it, whatever it's missing goes on the Want List **for** this deck ([[want-list]]), and the swap options go in its sideboard. Then **Suggested Tokens** opens for the new deck: tick, **Print Ticked…** and/or **Add Ticked** (Cancel skips it). A version of the deck is saved too (Edit → Decks → **Versions…**). Find it in Edit → Decks.

Note: The draft is a starting point. Open the new deck in Edit → Decks to change it like any other deck.
