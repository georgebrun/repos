# Build a Deck
> Edit → Decks → Build: a Commander deck drafted from your collection, every card with its reason.

## What you tell it
- **What can I build?…**: your commanders, best first, by how much of a deck your free cards already make for them: free spells in their colors, how far those go toward the guide, and (when EDHREC's page for the commander is saved) how many of its 100 most-played cards you have free. **Build This One** picks it. Tick **Include commanders whose copies are in a deck** to see those too.
- **Commander**: type to search. The list is A→Z and says which are free, yours but in use, or not owned.
- **Second commander** (shown when the commander can have one): Partner, Partner with, Friends forever, Choose a Background, or Doctor's companion. Only cards that can pair with the first are listed. **✕** for none. Both commanders' colors count.
- **Bracket**: 1 Exhibition to 5 cEDH (default 3). It keeps to the bracket: Game Changers (none in 1–2, up to 3 in 3), no mass land denial or chained extra turns below 4, and no two-card infinite combos below 4.
- **Cards to use**:
  - **Only my collection's free copies** (default): nothing to buy. Copies a deck or the Trade Binder already uses aren't touched.
  - **My collection + cards to get**: also cards you don't own, up to **Spend up to $** in all. They go on the Want List when you accept.
- **Deck worth at most $** (optional): the whole deck's value, your cards included.
- **Use the guide**: about how many of each kind of card: lands, ramp, card draw, removal, board wipes, protection, counterspells, tutors, recursion and finishers. Only a guide: change any number, untick any line, or untick **Use the guide** for just the best cards.

## The draft
Click **Build Draft**. While it works, a bar shows what it's doing; **Cancel** (or Esc) stops it and keeps the last draft. The commander is first (both, with partners), then the rest. Each card shows:
- **Source**: **Free (yours)**, **To get $x**, or **Basic**.
- **Why**: what it does in the deck (the guide line it fills), where it comes from, how many of this commander's decks on EDHREC play it, and how it fits the commander.

Above the draft:
- The guide's lines, green when met, amber when short. "+9 more cards do it too" counts cards picked for other reasons that do that job as well.
- Notes: a role it couldn't fill, combos in the draft, and what it had to go on (your collection, card tags, EDHREC, combos).

It picks from what EDHREC says this commander's decks play, the commander's own themes, combos with cards already in, cards that do more than one job, mana cost, and your free copies first. Without card tags (Update Database), it reads what cards do from their rules text, which is rougher.

## Guided Build
**Guided Build…** builds it step by step: one step per guide line (Ramp, Card draw …), then **The deck's plan**. Each step lists the best options for that job with the reason for each, with what you've chosen so far already counted. The top ones are ticked to start; tick and untick what you like, then **Next** (**Back** to change an earlier step).
- Cards chosen in another step that do this job count toward it too.
- It won't let you go over the bracket's Game Changers or the deck's spell slots.
- **Finish — fill the rest** (any step): your choices are locked and the builder fills the rest, lands included.

## Change it
- Right-click cards → **Lock (always keep it)**: they stay in every rebuild.
- Right-click cards → **Exclude and rebuild**: they're left out, and the draft is built again.
- Change anything on the left and click **Build Draft** again. **Clear locks and excludes** starts over.

## Try it, then keep it
- **Statistics & Test Hands…**: Deck Statistics for the draft: mana, combos, bracket, EDHREC, and **Sample Hand** to draw test hands.
- **Rule 0 Sheet…**: what to tell the table before a game: commander, the bracket you aimed for and the bracket the cards show, Game Changers, two-card combos, extra turns, land denial, tutors, interaction, how it wins and the saltiest cards. **Copy** or **Save…** it as a text file.
- **Accept…**: name it, and BoE saves it as a new Commander deck: your free copies are claimed for it, and whatever it's missing goes on the Want List **for** this deck ([[want-list]]). Find it in Edit → Decks.

Note: The draft is a starting point. Open the new deck in Edit → Decks to change it like any other deck.
