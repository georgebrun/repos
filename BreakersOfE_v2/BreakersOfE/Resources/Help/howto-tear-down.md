# Take a Deck Apart
> Step by step: free a deck's cards.

## Free some cards, keep the deck
1. Click **Edit**, then **Decks → Deck → Collection**, and pick the deck.
2. Select the cards in the deck table.
3. Click **Free Copies** (or [Delete]). The copies go back to Available; the cards stay in the deck list.

## Take the whole deck apart
1. Open the deck on any deck page.
2. Click **Tear Down…** and confirm.

Every claimed copy goes back to Available, and the deck file moves to the **Deleted Decks** folder in your data folder.

Warning: Tear Down can't be undone on the page. To get the deck back, copy its file from Deleted Decks back into Decks.
