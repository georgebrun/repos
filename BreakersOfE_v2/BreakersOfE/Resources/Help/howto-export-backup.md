# Export or Back Up Your Collection
> Step by step: send a list out, or keep a full copy.

## Send a list to a website or app
1. Click **Edit**, then **Import / Export**, then **Export**.
2. **Export**: **My collection**, **A deck**, **Want List** or **Trade Binder**.
3. **as**: **Text list** for most websites, or **ManaBox CSV**.
4. Tick **Only copies I can use** to leave out cards claimed by decks or the Trade Binder.
5. **Copy to Clipboard** and paste it into the website, or **Save File…**.

## Keep a full backup
1. **Export**: **My collection**, **as**: **BoE full backup**.
2. Click **Save File…** and keep the file somewhere safe (another drive, cloud storage).

To restore it: **Import**, **Format**: **BoE full collection file (.csv)**, **Replace (match the file)**, then **Open File…** and **Replace…**.

Tip: Copying your whole data folder while BoE is closed is a backup too. See [[data-folder]].
