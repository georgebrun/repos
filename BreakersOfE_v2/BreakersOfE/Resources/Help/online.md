# Online: MTGO and Arena
> Your Magic Online and Arena collections, kept apart from your paper cards.

**View → Online** has **MTGO Cards**, **MTGO Collection**, **Arena Cards** and **Arena Collection**. **Edit → Online → Collection → MTGO / Arena** changes them, the same way as [[edit-collection]].

- Online collections are separate from your paper collection and never mixed with it.
- They have no language, condition, storage or etched finish.
- **MTGO**: foil means Premium. Prices are in event tickets (tix), for non-foil copies.
- **Arena**: non-foil only, and no prices.

Note: Online collections can't be imported or exported yet.
