# Add a Set You Opened
> Step by step: packs, a box or a precon, straight from the set's cards.

1. In the side menu, click **Edit**, then **Sets**.
2. Find the release: scroll, or type its name in **Search**. Click its tile.
3. On the release's page, click the part you opened (the set, its Commander decks, its tokens …).
4. Set **Add/Remove Qty**, **Language** and **Condition** in the bar on top.
5. For each card you got, click **+** in the box under its picture: the white box is non-foil, the gold ones are foil (**F**) and etched (**E**).
6. Got the tokens too? Click **◀** to go back to the release's page and open its **Tokens**. They go to Collection → Tokens by themselves.

Tip: **Missing Only** hides the cards you already own, so a new card is easy to spot. Cards you don't own are dimmed; a card brightens as soon as you add it.

Tip: Clicked the wrong one? **−** takes it back, or **Undo** ([Ctrl+Z]).
