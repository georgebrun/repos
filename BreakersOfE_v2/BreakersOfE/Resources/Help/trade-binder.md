# Trade Binder
> Edit → Lists → Collection → Trade Binder: cards set aside to trade or sell.

Your collection is on top and the Trade Binder below. Copies you put in the binder come from one exact row of your collection. They count as **Used**, so no deck can take them; taking them out makes them **Available** again.

## Using the binder
- **Add to Trade Binder** ([Enter]): puts Qty free copies of the selected collection rows in the binder.
- **Remove from Trade Binder** ([Delete]): back to Available in your collection. [Shift+Delete] removes all of the selected binder rows.
- **Traded / Sold…**: the copies are gone: out of the binder **and** your collection. BoE asks first, and Undo can bring them back.

## Prices
- **Trade Value** = market price × your **Trade %** (70% to start): roughly what a shop pays in trade credit. Change it in Settings → Collection defaults.
- **Asking**: your own price per copy. Double-click the Asking cell (or right-click → **Set Asking Price…**) and type a price like 4.50; leave it empty for none. **Clear Asking Price** removes it.
- **Notes**: double-click the Notes cell, or right-click → **Edit Notes…**.

**Undo** or [Ctrl+Z] takes back the last 20 changes. See also [[howto-trade-binder]].
