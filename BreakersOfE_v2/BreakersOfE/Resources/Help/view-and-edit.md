# View and Edit
> The two sections of the side menu: looking, and changing.

The side menu has two sections. Only one is open at a time; click **View** or **Edit** to switch.

## View (look, don't change)
- **Card Pool**: every card Scryfall knows, by kind: Cards, Tokens, Planes, Schemes, Vanguards, Art Series, Conspiracies, Oversized, Front Cards. See [[card-pool]].
- **Sets**: every set as a tile with how much of it you own. See [[sets]].
- **Collection**: the cards you own, by the same kinds, plus **Trade Binder** and **Want List**.
- **Decks**: your decks as tiles, grouped by deck type (Commander, Constructed, …). Click one to open it.
- **Online**: MTGO Cards, MTGO Collection, Arena Cards, Arena Collection. See [[online]].

## Edit (change)
- **Pool → Collection**: add cards from the pool to your collection, by kind. See [[edit-collection]].
- **Sets**: add a set's cards from its pictures, one set at a time. See [[sets]].
- **Decks**: **Pool → Deck**, **Collection → Deck** and **Deck → Collection**. See [[decks]].
- **Lists**: **Collection → Trade Binder** and **Pool → Want List**. See [[trade-binder]] and [[want-list]].
- **Import / Export**: bring lists in, send them out. See [[import-export]].
- **Online → Collection**: your MTGO and Arena collections.

## Grid or Gallery
The **Grid / Gallery** switch at the top of the side menu shows cards as a table or as pictures across the app. On the Edit pages each table has its own **Show as Gallery** / **Show as Grid** button instead, remembered per table.

The picture itself stays clean. Under it, each finish the card comes in has a column:
- On top, the price of **one** copy. Hover it for the total, like **2 × $0.25 = $0.50**.
- Below, a box with how many: copies you own (card pool), copies in the deck (decks), or the row's quantity (your collection, Trade Binder, Want List).
- White is non-foil. Gold is foil (**1F**) and etched (**1E**).
- On the Edit pages the box has **−** and **+**: they take away or add **Qty** copies in that finish. On a collection row's picture they work on that row. The View pages show the count only.

Note: A finish you own always shows, even when Scryfall says the printing doesn't come in it. MTGO prices are in tix; Arena has no prices. Tokens and other tables without prices show the count only.

Note: A table's columns, order, sizes and zoom are shared between View and Edit: arrange the Collection table in View and Edit's collection table looks the same. See [[columns-and-layout]].
