# Columns, Layout and Zoom
> Set up each table the way you like it.

## Columns
- **Columns** opens a checklist: tick the columns to show. **Name** always stays. **Reset to default** puts the table back as it came.
- Drag a column header to move it; drag the edge of a header to resize it.
- **Legality** opens a checklist of formats. Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Commander and Pauper show at first; the rest are there to tick. An open deck shows one **Legal** column for its own format instead.

Everything is saved per table and **shared between View and Edit**: the Collection table looks the same in both.

## Zoom
In Grid view, hold [Ctrl] and turn the mouse wheel, or use [Ctrl+=] / [Ctrl+-], to zoom from 70% to 150%. [Ctrl+0] goes back to 100%. The status line shows the zoom. The Gallery has a **Card size** slider instead.

## The card panel and splitters
The card panel on the left can be made wider or narrower by dragging its edge. On the Edit pages you can also drag the bar between the two tables. BoE remembers the sizes.

## The totals row and status line
Collection tables and decks get a green **Totals** row in the grid. The status line under a table shows how many cards there are, and how many pass the filters.
