# Scan Cards with Your Phone
> Step by step: scan with a free phone app (ManaBox), then import into BoE.

BoE doesn't scan cards itself: free phone apps already do it well, on iPhone and Android, and BoE reads their files. **ManaBox** is free and reads the exact printing and finish. (Delver Lens and Dragon Shield work too.)

## On the phone
1. Install **ManaBox** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Scan your cards with its camera. Check each one: the printing (set and number) and **Foil** if it's foil, since a camera can't always tell.
3. Open the list you scanned into, choose **Export**, and pick **CSV**.
4. Save the file to your cloud drive (OneDrive, Google Drive, iCloud Drive, Dropbox), or email it to yourself.

## In BoE
1. Click **Edit**, then **Import / Export**, and stay on **Import**.
2. **Import into**: **Collection (cards and tokens)** or **Want List**.
3. Leave **Format** on **Auto-detect**: it finds **ManaBox (.csv)** by itself.
4. Click **Open File…**, pick the file, look over the preview and click **Import**.

Every line carries Scryfall's own ID, so each card comes in as the exact printing you scanned, with its finish, condition and language. Prices come from BoE's card data, not the app's.

Tip: For the **Trade Binder**, import into the **Collection** first: binder cards are copies from your collection. Then move them in with [[howto-trade-binder|the Trade Binder steps]].

Tip: **Undo Last Import** takes it all back if something looks wrong.
