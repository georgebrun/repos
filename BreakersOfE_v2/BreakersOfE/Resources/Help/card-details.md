# Card Details
> The card panel, and the card window.

## The card panel
Click a card in any table to see it in the panel on the left: the picture, name, mana cost, type, set, collector number, rarity, power and toughness (or loyalty / defense), rules and flavor text, artist, the finishes it comes in (**AVAILABLE AS**), today's prices and the decks you use it in.

**TAGS** says what the card does, like ramp, removal or card draw: Scryfall's card tags, downloaded with Update Database. Hover a tag for what it means.

**SALT** is the card's score on EDHREC's yearly salt vote (0–4: how much Commander players dislike playing against it) and its rank, or **Low** when it's milder than the list BoE keeps.

**LINKS** opens the card on **EDHREC**, **Scryfall** (this printing) or **Commander Spellbook** (combos with it) in your browser. On the Edit pages, right-click a card → **Open on** does the same.

## The card window
Double-click a card (a row or a gallery tile) for the card window. On the View pages, double-clicking the picture in the panel works too.
- **PRICE**: today's price and a chart of the last few prices. Each day you run Update Database, that day's prices are saved, so the chart grows over time.
- **IN YOUR DECKS**: every deck using the card, any printing. Opened from a deck, it lists your other decks.
- **INFORMATION**, **ORACLE TEXT**, **CARD TAGS** and **FLAVOR TEXT**.
- **📜 Rulings**: the official rulings for the card.
- **LINKS** and **SALT**: the card on EDHREC, Scryfall and Commander Spellbook, and its salt score.
- **SET** and **FORMAT LEGALITY**: the formats it's legal (green) or restricted (blue) in.

Use **◀** and **▶** (or the [←] and [→] keys) to go to the previous or next card in the table. [Esc] closes the window.

## Double-faced cards
Transforming and modal double-faced cards show like a regular card: the front side's name, type, cost, stats and text. **🔄 Show Back Face** flips the picture and the text to the back side; the button then reads **Show Front Face**. Split and adventure cards show both halves together, as printed.

## Pictures
A card back shows while a picture loads. If there is no picture (you're offline and it isn't kept, or Scryfall has none), the **Scryfall Fail** card shows instead. Settings → Card pictures can keep pictures for offline use: see [[settings]].
