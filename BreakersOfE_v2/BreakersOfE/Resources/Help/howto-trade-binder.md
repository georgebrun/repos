# Put Cards in the Trade Binder
> Step by step: set cards aside to trade or sell.

1. Click **Edit**, then **Lists → Collection → Trade Binder**.
2. Set **Qty**.
3. Select the row in your collection on top and click **Add to Trade Binder** (or [Enter]). Those copies are now set aside: no deck can take them.
4. To set a price, double-click the card's **Asking** cell in the binder, type a price like 4.50 and press [Enter].
5. When a card is traded or sold, select it in the binder and click **Traded / Sold…**. It leaves the binder and your collection.

Tip: **Trade Value** shows about what a shop would give in trade credit: market price × your Trade % (Settings → Collection defaults).
