# Import Cards from Another App
> Step by step: ManaBox, Moxfield, Archidekt, a text list…

1. In the other app, export your collection or deck as a **CSV** or **text list**.
2. In BoE, click **Edit**, then **Import / Export**, and stay on **Import**.
3. **Import into**: **Collection (cards and tokens)**, **Want List** or **A new deck**.
4. Leave **Format** on **Auto-detect**.
5. For the collection, keep **Add to what I have**, and pick the language and condition for lines that don't say.
6. Click **Open File…** and pick the file. (For a list on screen, a website or a chat, copy it and click **Paste Text**, then **Read Pasted Text**.)
7. Look over the preview. Lines marked **Check** matched more than one printing: change the **Printing** if BoE picked the wrong one. Lines left out say why.
8. Click **Import** and confirm.

Tip: **Undo Last Import** takes it all back if something looks wrong.

Tip: An imported deck comes in with nothing claimed. To tie it to your cards, open it in **Decks → Deck → Collection** and click **Whole Deck: Use Copies I Own…**.
