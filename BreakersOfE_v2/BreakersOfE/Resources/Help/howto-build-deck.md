# Build a Deck from Your Collection
> Step by step: a deck that uses the cards you own.

1. Click **Edit**, then **Decks → Collection → Deck**.
2. Click **New Deck…**. Type a **Name**, pick the **Deck type** (and the **Format** for Constructed) and click **Create**.
3. For a Commander deck: find your commander in the collection on top, right-click it and pick **Add as Commander**.
4. Find each card in your collection on top and click **Add Non-Foil** (or press [Enter]). Adding from a collection row claims those copies for this deck.
5. If an add breaks a rule (too many copies, not legal, wrong colors), **Add Anyway?** tells you why. **Cancel**, or **Add Anyway** if you mean it.
6. Click **Statistics** to check the mana curve, colors and draw odds.

The deck saves as you go. The line under the buttons shows how much of it is claimed from your collection.

Tip: Cards you don't own yet? Build in **Pool → Deck** instead, then use [[howto-find-missing]] to see what you need.

## Let BoE draft it
1. Click **Edit**, then **Decks → Build**.
2. Pick the **Deck type**: Commander, or 60-card (then the **Format** and the **Colors**).
3. Not sure what to build? Click **What can I build?…**: your commanders (and partner pairs), or for 60-card the color combinations, ranked by how much of a deck your free cards already make. **Build This One** picks it.
4. For Commander: pick the **Commander** (and a second one if it has Partner or a Background) and the **Bracket**. For 60-card: add **Key cards** to build around and **Plan words**.
5. Choose **Only my collection's free copies**, or **My collection + cards to get** with a spend limit.
6. Click **Build Draft** (or **Guided Build…** to pick the cards for each job yourself). Every card says why it's in.
7. Right-click cards to **Lock** or **Exclude** them and build again until you like it. **Statistics & Test Hands…** shows the mana and draws test hands.
8. Click **Accept…** and name the deck. Your free copies are claimed, the rest go on the Want List for it, the swap options go in its sideboard, and you can add its tokens.
