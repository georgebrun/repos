# What's New in 2.1.2
> Your Trade Binder and Want List on your phone; 2.1.1's two-sided tokens; and 2.1.0's deck builder, with card tags, combos, EDHREC, salt, a Want List per deck, print slips and deck versions.

## 2.1.2: Phone lists
Your Trade Binder and Want List on your phone, iPhone or Android, for the store or a trade. See [[phone-lists]].
- **📱 Phone…** on the Trade Binder and Want List (or **Settings → Phone lists**): BoE keeps a file, **BoE Lists.json**, in your cloud drive (OneDrive, Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox) up to date by itself as your collection changes.
- The **BoE Lists** page on your phone opens it: pictures, the full set name, search, filters by color or deck, prices and your asking prices. Scan the QR code in Settings, add it to your Home Screen, and it opens like an app, even without signal.
- Only the Trade Binder and Want List go in the file, never the rest of your collection or your decks. Free, with no account to make.

## 2.1.1: Two-sided tokens
Many tokens have another token on the back, and Scryfall lists each side as its own token. Now BoE knows they're one card. See [[two-sided-tokens]].
- **Adding a token asks what's on its other side**: the card pool's tokens of that release, all its token sets. Pick it and both go in, paired; or **Single-Sided**. Tokens from before two-sided ones existed (2012) don't ask.
- Each side keeps its own row, showing the other: **Treasure // Angel**. The totals count each card once.
- A deck that takes one side takes the card: the other side shows as **Used**, with the deck in "Used in".
- **Show Other Side** in the card panel and the card window.
- Removing one side removes the other; changing finish, language or condition moves both. [Ctrl+Z] puts both back, paired.
- **Two-Sided…** and right-click → **Other Side…** / **Unpair** to fix a pair; **Export / Import Two-Sided Pairs…** to share them.
- **Suggested Tokens** finds a token on either side of your cards (and either face of a double-faced token), with a **Prefer** choice; one card that's both tokens a deck makes is used for both. Print slips print both sides, or one.
- Guided Build: a pick past the guide's target says "over the guide".

## 2.1.0: Build a deck
**Edit → Decks → Build** drafts a deck from your collection, with a reason for every card. See [[build]].
- **Commander** (with partners and Backgrounds) or **60-card** for a format you pick: Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper and more.
- **Only your free copies**, or your collection plus cards to get within a spend limit; an optional cap on the whole deck's value.
- Aims for the **Commander Bracket** you pick (Game Changers, two-card combos, land denial, extra turns).
- A **guide** (lands, ramp, card draw, removal …) you can change or switch off; 60-card decks also get a **mana curve**, **key cards** and **plan words**.
- **Guided Build** lets you pick the cards for each job yourself. **What can I build?** ranks your commanders, partner pairs or color combinations by how much of a deck you already own.
- **Swap options**: the cards that just missed go in the sideboard, unclaimed, to swap in after testing.
- **Accept** makes the deck: your free copies claimed, the rest on the Want List for it, its tokens offered, a version saved.
- **Rule 0 Sheet** for the table, from the draft or from Deck Statistics.

## 2.1.0: Decks
- **Versions…**: save a version with a note, compare versions, restore one. See [[decks]].
- **Tear Down** asks what to do with the deck's Want List cards: keep them for the Collection or remove them.
- **Move to Main / Move to Sideboard** claim and free copies, so the sideboard can hold swap options.
- **Deck Statistics** has new **Combos** and **EDHREC** tabs, the **saltiest cards** on Overview, and a real two-card combo check on **Bracket**. See [[statistics]].
- View → Decks counts the main deck only; a sideboard shows on the tile's second line.

## 2.1.0: Cards
- **Tags** (Scryfall's card tags), **Links** (EDHREC, Scryfall, Commander Spellbook) and **Salt** in the card panel and card window. Right-click → **Open on ▸** on Edit pages. See [[card-details]].
- **Update Database** now also downloads card tags, Commander Spellbook's combos and EDHREC's salt scores. See [[update-database]].

## 2.1.0: Want List
- Every want is **for** a deck or for your **Collection** (the **For** column). See [[want-list]].
- **Got It** puts the copies in your collection and claims them for the decks they were for.
- Add to the Want List from Edit → Collection and from a deck's missing cards.

## 2.1.0: Print slips
- Print **token slips** from Suggested Tokens, and card slips (marked PROXY) from the Want List and a deck's table.

## 2.1.0: Fixes
- Export leaves Oversized cards out unless you tick them.
- A second Update Database right after the first no longer fails while the keyword list is still being rebuilt.
