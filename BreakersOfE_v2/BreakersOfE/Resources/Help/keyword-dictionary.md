# Keyword Dictionary
> What every keyword means, the official rules, and the cards that have it.

Click **Keyword Dictionary** in the side menu.

- The search box at the top finds any part of a keyword's name (hors… finds Horsemanship). The category list narrows the list further.
- Pick a keyword to see **WHAT IT MEANS**, the **OFFICIAL RULES** from the Comprehensive Rules, an **EXAMPLE CARD**, and the cards that have it.
- **Show in Pool** and **Show in My Collection** open the card table filtered to that keyword.
- **Get the Official Rules** downloads Wizards' Comprehensive Rules (Update Database does too); later it reads **Check for Newer Rules**.
