# Two-Sided Tokens
> Two tokens printed on one card: pair them, and BoE treats them as one card.

Many tokens have another token printed on the back: Commander decks have had them since 2014, booster packs since Modern Horizons (2019), and Reality Fracture's tokens are all two-sided. Scryfall lists each side as its own token and doesn't say which two are printed together (booster pairings vary, and decks differ), so you tell BoE. A token Scryfall already has as one double-faced card (Incubator // Phyrexian) is one card already and needs nothing.

## Adding a two-sided token
1. In **Edit → Pool → Collection → Tokens**, find the token in the pool (on top) and add it as usual: [Enter], [Shift+Enter], or **Add Non-Foil / Add Foil**.
2. The **Other Side** window lists the card pool's tokens of that release (all its token sets). Untick the release box to see every token; **Find** narrows the list.
3. Pick the token on the back and click **Add to Collection** (or double-click it). Both go in as two rows, paired, in the same finish, language and condition.
4. No other side: **Single-Sided** ([Enter]). **Cancel** ([Esc]) adds nothing.

Tokens released before Dark Ascension (February 2012, the first two-sided tokens) don't ask. Adding more of a token you've paired before opens with the same other side picked. Adding several selected tokens at once doesn't ask: pair them afterwards.

## How a pair shows
- Each side keeps its own row, sorted under its own name: **Treasure // Angel** and **Angel // Treasure**. The name itself stays Treasure (sorting, filters and exports use it).
- The totals line counts each card once: "Totals (19 rows = 10 cards)".
- The card panel and the card window have **Show Other Side**: the token on the back, then back again.
- A deck's Tokens part shows the other side too.

## Decks
A deck that takes one side takes the card: the other row shows it as **Used**, its "Used in" list says *Other side (Angel)* and the deck, and no other deck can claim it. The same deck can hold both sides of one card (one side out at a time).

## Fixing, unpairing, removing
- Select a token row and click **Two-Sided…** (or right-click → **Other Side…**) to pair it with the token on its back — from your tokens or the card pool (one you don't have is added) — or to unpair it.
- Right-click → **Unpair** takes a pair apart: both rows stay, each its own card.
- Removing copies of one side removes the other side with them (one card, both rows); changing finish, language or condition moves both. Single copies go first, and copies in use by decks are kept. BoE asks first, and [Ctrl+Z] brings both back, paired.

## Sharing pairs
Right-click → **Export Two-Sided Pairs…** saves your pairs as a CSV file (names, set codes, collector numbers, cards). **Import Two-Sided Pairs…** reads one (yours or a friend's), lists the pairs you have both sides of, and pairs them when you say so. Check them against your cards: pairings can differ.

## Suggested Tokens and print slips
- **Suggested Tokens…** finds a token on either side of your two-sided cards, and on either face of a double-faced token. **Prefer** picks which of your printings is offered first when you have one both on its own and on a two-sided card (remembered).
- When one card of yours is both tokens a deck makes, it says *one card with …*: that card is used for both, one side at a time (Add Ticked puts both in the deck; it prints once).
- Print slips of a two-sided token print both sides (Side 1 of 2, Side 2 of 2); tick **Print one side only** for just the one. With both rows of one card selected, the card prints once.
