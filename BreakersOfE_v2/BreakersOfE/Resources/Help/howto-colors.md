# Change the Theme or Make Your Own Colors
> Step by step: Light, Dark, or your own.

1. Click **Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, pick **Dark**, **Light** or **Follow Windows**. The app changes right away.

## Your own colors
1. Pick **Custom Colors**.
2. Click **New Preset** and give it a **Name**.
3. **Start from** Dark or Light.
4. For each color, click its swatch and pick one, or type a #RRGGBB value. **Default** puts one color back.
5. If BoE shows an amber line about contrast, that text would be hard to read: pick a lighter or darker color.

**Reset All Colors** puts the whole preset back to its start.

Note: The card tables keep their light rows in every theme, so card colors always read the same.
