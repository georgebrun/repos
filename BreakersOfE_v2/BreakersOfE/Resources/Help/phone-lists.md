# Phone Lists
> Your Trade Binder and Want List on your phone, for the store or a trade.

BoE keeps your Trade Binder and Want List in a file, **BoE Lists.json**, in your cloud drive. The **BoE Lists** page on your phone opens it: pictures, search, filters by color or deck, prices and your asking prices. It works the same on iPhone and Android, and keeps the lists on the phone for when there's no signal. It costs nothing, and the lists stay in your own cloud drive.

## On the PC (once)
1. Click **📱 Phone…** on the Trade Binder or Want List (in View or Edit), or open **Settings → Phone lists**, and tick **Keep my Trade Binder and Want List on my phone**.
2. The file goes to **OneDrive\Breakers of E** when your PC has OneDrive. For Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox, click **Change Folder…** and pick a folder in it.

From then on BoE writes the file again by itself whenever your collection changes (an edit, Got It, a deck claiming cards, an import). **Update Now** writes it straight away.

## On the phone (once)
1. Point the phone's camera at the QR code in Settings (or open the address under it).
2. Add the page to your Home Screen: on iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen**; on Android, **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It opens like an app from then on.
3. Open it, tap **Open my lists file**, and pick **BoE Lists.json** in your cloud drive.

## Newer lists
Tap **Update** on the phone and pick the file again: your cloud drive gives the newest one BoE wrote.

Tip: the page is the same for everyone and holds nobody's cards; yours come only from your file.

Note: only the Trade Binder and Want List go in the file, never the rest of your collection or your decks.
