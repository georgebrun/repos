# Phone Lists
> Your Trade Binder and Want List on your phone, for the store or a trade.

BoE keeps your Trade Binder and Want List in a file, **BoE Lists.json**. The **BoE Lists** page on your phone shows it: pictures, search, filters by color or deck, prices and your asking prices. It works the same on iPhone and Android, and keeps the lists on the phone for when there's no signal. It costs nothing.

There are two ways to get the file to the phone:
- **Dropbox (hands-free).** BoE sends the lists to your Dropbox whenever your collection changes, and the phone page gets them by itself each time you open it. No picking files, no Update. The Dropbox app isn't needed on the PC or the phone.
- **A cloud drive folder.** BoE writes the file to a folder in OneDrive, Google Drive, iCloud Drive or Dropbox on your PC, and on the phone you pick the file (tap **Update** and pick it again for newer lists).

## On the PC (once)
1. Click **📱 Phone…** on the Trade Binder or Want List (in View or Edit), or open **Settings → Phone lists**, and tick **Keep my Trade Binder and Want List on my phone**.
2. For hands-free: click **Connect Dropbox…**. Dropbox's page opens in your browser: sign in and click **Allow**. BoE only gets its own folder in your Dropbox (**Apps › Breakers of E Lists**), never your other files.
3. Or, without Dropbox: the file goes to **OneDrive\Breakers of E** when your PC has OneDrive. For another cloud drive, click **Change Folder…** and pick a folder in it.

From then on BoE sends the lists again by itself whenever your collection changes (an edit, Got It, a deck claiming cards, an import). **Update Now** sends them straight away.

## No Dropbox yet?
Click **Free Dropbox account** (under Connect Dropbox…), or go to dropbox.com/register.
1. Sign up with your name, email and a password. **Dropbox Basic** is free (2 GB; the lists take a few hundred KB).
2. Confirm the email Dropbox sends.
3. Skip any offer of a paid trial or of installing the Dropbox app: BoE doesn't need either.
4. Back in BoE, click **Connect Dropbox…**.

## On the phone (once)
1. Point the phone's camera at the QR code in Settings (or open the address under it).
2. Add the page to your Home Screen: on iPhone, **Share → Add to Home Screen**; on Android, **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It opens like an app from then on.
3. Open it and tap **Sign in with Dropbox**, with the same Dropbox as BoE, and **Allow**. (Without Dropbox: tap **Open my lists file** and pick **BoE Lists.json** in your cloud drive.)

## Newer lists
- **Dropbox:** nothing to do. The page gets the newest lists each time you open it or come back to it; **Refresh** gets them right now. With no signal it shows the lists kept on the phone.
- **Cloud drive folder:** tap **Update** and pick the file again: your cloud drive gives the newest one BoE wrote.

## Stop or switch
- **Disconnect** in Settings → Phone lists stops sending to Dropbox (BoE goes back to the cloud drive folder). On the phone, **Sign out of Dropbox** at the bottom of the page.
- You can also remove BoE in Dropbox itself: **Settings → Apps** (connected apps) on dropbox.com.

Tip: the page is the same for everyone and holds nobody's cards; yours come only from your own Dropbox or your own file.

Note: only the Trade Binder and Want List go in the file, never the rest of your collection or your decks.
