# Statistics
> Your collection and your decks in numbers.

## Collection Statistics
On **Collection → Cards**, click **Statistics**. It covers the rows on screen, so clear the filters for the whole collection. It shows: card counts, unique printings and names, foils, total and average value, cards used in decks and available; **Collection value over time**; breakdowns by color, rarity and card type; the top sets by value; and the 25 most valuable cards.

## Deck Statistics
With a deck open (View → Decks, or an Edit deck page), click **Statistics**. It always covers the whole deck.
- **Overview:** color identity, counts (main deck, sideboard, lands, creatures, other spells), average mana value, deck value, and bars by color, type, rarity and edition.
- **Mana:** the mana curve, the lands, and **symbols needed vs. sources**: for each color, how much of the deck needs it and how many sources make it.
- **Draw Odds:** tick the cards to look for; see the chance of drawing them in the opening hand and on turns 1–10, on the play or on the draw.
- **Sample Hand:** **New Hand**, **Mulligan** and **Draw a Card**.
- **Bracket** (Commander decks): the likely bracket, with Game Changers, mass land denial and extra turns.
- **Format Check:** the deck's rule checks, and whether it's legal in each format.
