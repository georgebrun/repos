# Statistics
> Your collection and your decks in numbers.

## Collection Statistics
On **Collection → Cards**, click **Statistics**. It covers the rows on screen, so clear the filters for the whole collection. It shows: card counts, unique printings and names, foils, total and average value, cards used in decks and available; **Collection value over time**; breakdowns by color, rarity and card type; the top sets by value; and the 25 most valuable cards.

## Deck Statistics
With a deck open (View → Decks, or an Edit deck page), click **Statistics**. It always covers the whole deck.
- **Overview:** color identity, counts (main deck, sideboard, lands, creatures, other spells), average mana value, deck value, and bars by color, type, rarity and edition, and the deck's **saltiest cards**: the 10 highest scores on EDHREC's salt vote (0–4, how much Commander players dislike facing a card), worth mentioning before a game.
- **Mana:** the mana curve, the lands, and **symbols needed vs. sources**: for each color, how much of the deck needs it and how many sources make it.
- **Draw Odds:** tick the cards to look for; see the chance of drawing them in the opening hand and on turns 1–10, on the play or on the draw.
- **Sample Hand:** **New Hand**, **Mulligan** and **Draw a Card**.
- **Combos:** combos from Commander Spellbook (downloaded with Update Database).
  - **In the deck:** combos the main deck already has, commander included.
  - **One card away:** combos the deck is one card short of, in its colors. Each says whether that card is free in your collection (no deck or the Trade Binder has claimed it), owned but all in use, or not owned. Free ones come first; tick **Only cards I have free copies of** to see just those.
  - Each combo shows what it does, its cards, anything else it needs (like "a creature with power 3 or more", which isn't checked), Spellbook's bracket tag and how many EDHREC decks run it. **How it works** opens the steps; **Commander Spellbook ↗** opens the combo's page.
- **EDHREC** (decks with a commander): the cards EDHREC recommends for your commander, and where you stand on each.
  - **Decks**: the share of this commander's decks on EDHREC that run the card. **Synergy**: how much more this commander's decks run it than other decks of its colors.
  - **You**: **In the deck**, **free in your collection** (unclaimed copies), **own, all in use**, or **not owned**.
  - **Show** narrows to cards not in the deck, free ones, or ones in the deck; **Section** to one of EDHREC's lists (High Synergy Cards, Creatures, Lands …). Click a header to sort; double-click a card to open it on EDHREC.
  - The first time you open a commander it's fetched from EDHREC and saved; after 7 days, or with **Refresh**, it's fetched again. If EDHREC can't be reached, the saved copy shows with a note, or the tab says EDHREC is unavailable.
- **Bracket** (Commander decks): the likely bracket, with Game Changers, mass land denial, extra turns and two-card combos. A two-card infinite or game-winning combo means at least Bracket 3; whether it can come together early (Bracket 4) is up to you.
- **Format Check:** the deck's rule checks, and whether it's legal in each format.
