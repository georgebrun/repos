# The Card Pool
> Every card there is, from Scryfall.

The card pool is every card in Scryfall's data, split by kind: **Cards**, **Tokens**, **Planes**, **Schemes**, **Vanguards**, **Art Series** and **Conspiracies**. It comes from [[update-database]]; until the first update the pool is empty.

## What a row shows
Each row is one printing: a card in one set with one collector number. Columns include the name, cost, type, set and set symbol, rarity, prices, how many you own, and more. Pick the ones you want with **Columns**: see [[columns-and-layout]].

- Text is in the card's color: white, blue, black, red or green; gold for two or more colors; grey for colorless; brown for lands.
- Prices are per finish: **USD** (non-foil), **Foil $** and **Etched $**.
- **Legality** adds a column per format (Legal, Res, Ban, No).

## Finding cards
- **Search:** type the start of a card name; BoE jumps to the first match.
- **Filters:** the funnel on each column, or the **Filters** panel for color, rarity, type, format, price ranges, keywords and more.

See [[search-and-filters]].

## Looking closer
Click a card to see it in the panel on the left. Double-click it for the card window: prices and price history, the decks you use it in, rulings and every format it's legal in. See [[card-details]].

Tip: Double-faced cards show one side at a time. **🔄 Show Back Face** flips the picture and the text.
