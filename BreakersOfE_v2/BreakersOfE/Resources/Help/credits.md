# Credits
> Where BoE's card data comes from, and Wizards of the Coast's Fan Content Policy.

## Scryfall
Card data, prices, rulings, card tags, symbols and pictures come from **Scryfall** (scryfall.com), through its free bulk data and API. BoE isn't made by or connected with Scryfall.

## Commander Spellbook
Combos come from **Commander Spellbook** (commanderspellbook.com), the combo database for Magic, through its free bulk file. BoE isn't made by or connected with Commander Spellbook.

## EDHREC
Commander recommendations (the EDHREC tab in Deck Statistics), salt scores and card links come from **EDHREC** (edhrec.com). EDHREC has no official API: BoE reads the same files its pages use, keeps each commander's for 7 days, and asks again only when you open a commander that's older or click Refresh. Salt scores are downloaded with Update Database. BoE isn't made by or connected with EDHREC.

## Printed slips and proxies
**Print Slip** and **Print Ticked…** make plain paper slips to sleeve over a card: name, cost, type, rules text and power/toughness, with no card art, no frame and no set. Each carries "© Wizards of the Coast LLC" in place of the artist and set.
- **Tokens** print plain.
- **Cards** print as proxies: **PROXY** across the picture box and "Proxy · Not for tournament play" at the bottom. The Magic Tournament Rules ask that any stand-in card be clearly and conspicuously marked as a proxy, and show at least the card's name and mana cost.
- **Tournaments:** players may not make their own proxies for sanctioned play, Friday Night Magic included. Only the Head Judge issues one, for a card damaged during that event.
- **Casual games:** Wizards has said it doesn't police playtest cards made for personal, non-commercial use that aren't reproductions of real cards. The official Commander rules expect real cards, so use proxies only when your playgroup agrees before the game.
- Slips are never for sale.
