# Settings
> Your choices, saved as you make them.

## Appearance
**Dark**, **Light**, **Follow Windows** (switches when Windows does) or **Custom Colors**. The card tables keep their light rows in every theme.

**Custom Colors** keeps presets of your own: pick a **Preset**, **New Preset**, rename it, choose **Start from** Dark or Light, then set each color (window, panel, text, secondary text, accent, warning) with its swatch or a #RRGGBB value. BoE warns in amber when text would be hard to read. **Reset All Colors** puts the preset back. See [[howto-colors]].

## Collection defaults
- **Language** and **Condition** for new cards (English and Near Mint to start).
- **Trade %**: the share of market price a shop pays in trade credit (70% to start), used for the Trade Binder's Trade Value.

## Card pictures
- **Keep pictures of my cards** keeps the pictures of your collection, Trade Binder, Want List and deck cards, so they show offline.
- **Also keep every picture I look at** keeps the pool's too (more space).
- **Get My Cards' Pictures Now**, **Download All Pictures…** (several GB), **Stop** and **Delete Kept Pictures…**.

## Startup and updates
- **Open on**: the page BoE starts on.
- **Remind me to update after**: when the update reminder appears (or Never).
- **Restart Tour**: shows the Program Tour again.

## Data folder
Shows where your data is and how big it is. **Open Folder**, **Move…** (see [[howto-move-folder]]) or **Use Documents\Breakers of E Again**. See [[data-folder]].
