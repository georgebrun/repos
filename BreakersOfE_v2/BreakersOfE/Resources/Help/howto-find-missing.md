# Find What a Deck Is Missing
> Step by step: claim what you own, and put the rest on the Want List.

1. Click **Edit**, then **Decks → Deck → Collection**, and pick the deck.
2. Click **Whole Deck: Use Copies I Own…**. The preview shows what you own (✓) and what's missing (✗). Click **Claim them**.
3. Click **Find Missing…**.
4. Under **You own another printing**, tick any card you're happy to use in a printing you already own; the deck switches to it and claims those copies.
5. Under **Add to the Want List**, tick the cards to look for.
6. Click **Apply Ticked**.

The missing cards are now on your Want List ([[want-list]]). When you get them, use **Got It…** there, then **Use Copies I Own** on the deck.

Tip: One **Undo** takes back the whole Find Missing step: the deck, its claims and the Want List.
