# Update Prices or the Card Database
> Step by step: keep prices and new cards current.

1. Click **Update Database** at the bottom of the side menu.
2. Click **Update Database** for everything (new cards, prices, symbols and rulings), or **Prices Only** for just today's prices (quicker).
3. Wait until it's done: **Update Database** ends with **Rulings download complete!**, **Prices Only** with **Price update complete!**. **Cancel** stops safely.

Your collection's prices and the price charts update with it. Nothing you entered is changed.

Tip: Set how often BoE reminds you in Settings → **Remind me to update after**.
