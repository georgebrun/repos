## Breakers of E 2.1.4

Two-sided tokens are one card again: each one has a row of its own, "Officer // Treasure", and goes into a deck as one token.

### What's new
- **A two-sided token card is one row** in Collection → Tokens, named with both sides in alphabetical order (**Officer // Treasure**). It has its own quantity, finish, language and condition, and its own Used and Available. Searching either side's name finds it.
- **Decks hold it as one token line** and claim it from that row, like any other card.
- **Adding a token** from the pool still asks what's on its back: pick it and the two-sided card goes in as one row, or choose **Single-Sided**.
- **Two-Sided…** (or right-click → **Other Side…**) makes two-sided cards from single tokens you have; right-click a two-sided card → **Unpair** takes it apart again. [Ctrl+Z] undoes either.
- **Your existing pairs are converted** the first time 2.1.4 starts. Each pair's copies move to a row of their own, and decks that held either side hold the card instead. Your collection and the decks it changes are backed up first (Backups › "Before two-sided cards …"), and BoE tells you what it did.

### Fixed
- A two-sided token you owned and hadn't used could refuse to go into a deck, because its free copy was counted on the other side's row.

### Install
Download **BreakersOfE_Setup_v2.1.4.exe** below and run it over your current version. Your collection, decks and phone lists stay as they are (two-sided pairs are converted, as above).
